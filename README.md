### 🧬 Research Focus: Embodied Intelligence
I am a researcher dedicated to bridging the gap between digital cognition and physical action.
* **Architectures:** Deep dive into **Transformer** and **Mamba-2 SSM** (State Space Models) for efficient long-context reasoning.
* **Models:** Actively exploring **World Models** and **VLA (Vision-Language-Action)** models to empower robots with "common sense."
* **Vision:** Crafting the "brain" for embodied agents that can perceive, reason, and interact with the complex real world.
